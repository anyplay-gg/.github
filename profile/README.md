# AnyPlay

Everything around [AnyPS5](https://github.com/boykopovar/AnyPS5) that players need: a launcher for your games, and an online network to play them with friends.

AnyPS5 turns PS5 game executables into native Windows and Linux programs. Its team works on the core: system libraries, the GPU and shaders. AnyPlay builds the rest.

| Project | What it is | Status |
|---|---|---|
| [launcher](https://github.com/anyplay-gg/launcher) | Finds your game dumps, unlocks them with AnyPS5 and starts them. Keeps controls, saves and trophies per game. Windows and Linux. | Released |
| [network](https://github.com/anyplay-gg/network) | Online play over the internet: accounts, friends, sessions, invitations and encrypted connections between players. | In development |
| [cloud](https://github.com/anyplay-gg/cloud) | The central services of the network. AnyPlay runs the main one; anyone can host their own. | In development |

## Ground rules

- We never connect to Sony's servers and never use Sony's code, keys or leaked documents.
- We don't stand in for a publisher's servers, and we don't bypass anti-cheat or account checks.
- Only games you own. Nothing here ships or downloads game files.
